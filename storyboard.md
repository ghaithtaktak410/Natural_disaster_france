# Storyboard - natural disasters (CatNat)

Target audience: anyone who has heard about "natural disaster decrees" in the news and wonders what the numbers really mean (no technical background needed).
Main message: a CatNat decree is an administrative decision, not a measure of the disaster.

| Part | Visual | Message | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | Text | Almost every commune is in the register, but one row = one decree for one commune | - |
| 1. Overview | 5 key figures + bars by year and peril | A few years dominate (1982, 1999), the last years are incomplete | Filters, log scale |
| 2. Map | Map of the departments | Where? Total or number of decrees per commune | 3 indicators, hover |
| 2. Comparisons | Bars by region + small charts | Which regions, which perils | Hover, filters |
| 2. Seasons | Coloured table month x peril | When do the perils happen | Hover |
| 2. Delays | Cumulative curves | How long before the decree | Hover, filters |
| 3. Quality | Figures + table of checks | Duplicates, fixed dates, incomplete years | - |
| 4. Conclusion | Text | Administrative register, ideas to go further | - |

## Video script (2 to 4 min)

1. 0:00 - 0:20: present the subject and the starting question (why is almost every commune in the register?).
2. 0:20 - 1:20: show the key figures and the chart by year, turn on the log scale, filter on drought, tick and untick the merge of clay.
3. 1:20 - 2:20: map (change the indicator), regions, seasons, delays.
4. 2:20 - 2:50: data quality part and limits.
5. 2:50 - 3:20: conclusion and ideas to go further.

Remember to check on screen that the numbers I say are the ones displayed when I record.
